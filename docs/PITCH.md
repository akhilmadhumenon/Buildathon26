# RaithaLine

Karnataka farmers already have a phone. RaithaLine calls that phone when a mandi is worth the trip, and texts the price it actually used.

The question is small: sell, delay the harvest, store, or send this crop this week. Ten crops are on the line. Tomato, onion, potato, dry chilli, banana, ragi, maize, tur, paddy, and groundnut. Sugarcane, cotton, coffee, and arecanut are off it, because those sales do not clear as a same-week APMC run.

Tomato and banana are not ragi. A soft price means leave the fruit on the vine for 3 to 4 days. A harvested load lasts 3 to 5 days without cold storage, so the line says sell, not hold. Ragi, tur, maize, dry chilli, groundnut, paddy, onion, and potato get a dry godown.

The spoken price is the modal and the day's min–max. Kolar tomato can be a few hundred rupees a quintal and a couple of thousand on the same morning, depending on grade. One modal, with no range, sends a farmer to pay for transport on the wrong crate.

The screen in the room is an FPO Aggregation Console, not a farmer app. An FPO already pools lots, books one truck, and can use a nearby-mandi gap. A farmer who took input credit from a local commission agent is not told to abandon that agent and drive to Bengaluru alone.

Agmarknet often arrives 24 to 48 hours late and can skip a weekend. The FPO manager types the live morning gate price at 7:00 AM. That number replaces the stale modal before the 8:00 AM call batch is approved. Arrivals and last year's week stay on the official series.

What we measured, and only this: a 7-day modal forecast for tomato at Kolar, checked on the latest 90 days against "tomorrow equals today" and "same week last year". The other nine crops use one visible rule, today's modal against the same week last year. Prices are the Agmarknet series stored on the machine. CEDA's cleaned download is the same feed behind a free key, and that key is not in this build, so we do not call it.

Climate is a species screen for all ten at Kolar and Kalaburagi. Today's temperature and rain are Open-Meteo normals for 2015–2024. The shift is WorldClim CMIP6 at the 10-minute cell, the mean of MIROC6 and MPI-ESM1-2-HR for 2041–2060 against 1970–2000. At these two cells that is about +0.8°C to +1.2°C, with rainfall only a few percent higher. It is not variety advice, and it is milder than a blanket +1.5°C to +2.5°C line. When a farmer marks the load in transit or completes the sale, the line speaks that screen for their crop against drought-hardy ragi and tur, before the next sowing.

A question about a spray or a fertiliser is answered only when a Karnataka Kisan Call Centre record for that crop matches the words. The line reads the record id. If nothing matches, it refuses and names the Krishi Vigyan Kendra.

What we will say in the room:

- The forecast is a prototype. If it does not beat both baselines, the slide says so.
- EcoCrop ranges are a screen. We do not name a variety.
- Location is the saved village, or coordinates when the gateway already has them. It is not a cell-tower fix.
- A dose question is refused. The line names the Krishi Vigyan Kendra.
- Nobody is called unless they opted in, the harvest window is open, they are not already on the road, and a person approves the row.

Sensor towers, drones, live news, and a smartphone app are the next build. This one is the phone call and the mandi series behind it.
