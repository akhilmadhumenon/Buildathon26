A soft price is two different jobs. Tomato and banana: delay harvest and leave the fruit on the vine for 3 to 4 days. Picked fruit lasts 3 to 5 days without cold storage, so a harvested load is a sell. Ragi, tur, maize, dry chilli, groundnut, paddy, onion, and potato: store in a dry godown.

Agmarknet often lands 24 to 48 hours late and can skip a weekend. The gate price replaces that modal for advice and for the calls approved after it. A nearby-mandi call is an FPO pooled trip. It does not ask a farmer to leave a commission agent who advanced input credit.
